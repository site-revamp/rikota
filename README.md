# RIKOTA Dental Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

RIKOTA Dental Clinic is a multi-location dental healthcare network providing general, cosmetic, restorative, orthodontic, surgical and paediatric dentistry. The clinic operates in Lviv, Ukraine, and Warsaw, Poland.

The redesigned homepage presents RIKOTA as a modern, technology-focused dental clinic, helping new and existing patients understand its treatments, explore its clinical capabilities and find relevant contact information.

The design follows the concept **"The Architecture of a Confident Smile"**, combining an editorial visual style, clear content structure and a professional healthcare-focused presentation.

## 2. Main Improvements

Three key problems identified in the original website were addressed through the redesign.

### 1. Unclear Service Discovery

**Previous issue:** Patients could find it difficult to identify relevant dental treatments and understand the available services.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Organised dental services into clearly defined treatment categories.
- Introduced dedicated treatment pathways for different patient needs.
- Added six custom SVG icons arranged in two rows of three to improve service recognition.

**Result:** Patients can navigate the main treatment categories more easily and understand the clinic's service offering.

### 2. Insufficient Trust and Clinical Credibility

**Previous issue:** The original homepage did not communicate the clinic's clinical expertise, technology and patient-focused approach prominently enough.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Introduced dedicated sections highlighting dental technology and clinical capabilities.
- Improved the presentation of specialist expertise and patient care.
- Added structured content explaining the treatment process.
- Incorporated relevant dental imagery and a more professional visual direction.

**Result:** The homepage communicates RIKOTA's professional capabilities and provides visitors with a clearer understanding of its approach to dental care.

### 3. Weak Conversion Pathways

**Previous issue:** Important contact and appointment actions were not sufficiently prominent or consistently presented throughout the homepage.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Introduced clearer appointment and contact calls to action.
- Improved the visibility of clinic location and contact information.
- Added a mobile sticky action bar with two dedicated options: **Call Clinic** and **Book a Visit**.
- Improved navigation to help visitors reach relevant information more efficiently.

**Result:** Visitors have clearer pathways to contact the clinic and enquire about appointments.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces several visual and usability improvements:

- **Colour scheme:** Established a clean visual identity using white (#FFFFFF) as the primary colour and warm stone beige (#CEC8BA) as the secondary colour.
- **Typography:** Used Space Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for body text to create a consistent typographic hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Introduced spacious sections, editorial typography and asymmetric layouts to improve content presentation.
- **Visual presentation:** Combined dental imagery, subtle technical details and custom SVG icons to create a cohesive healthcare-focused appearance.
- **Content consistency:** Standardised the English-language homepage content and improved the organisation of information.
- **Navigation:** Implemented responsive navigation for desktop and mobile experiences.
- **User experience:** Structured the homepage around treatments, clinical technology, the patient journey, frequently asked questions and clinic locations.
- **Mobile experience:** Improved smaller-screen usability with responsive layouts and prominent contact actions.

## 4. Final Outcome

The redesigned RIKOTA homepage provides a clearer presentation of the clinic's services, clinical expertise and locations.

By addressing service discovery, trust-building and conversion pathways, the new design creates a more organised and consistent experience across desktop and mobile devices.

The implementation focuses on improving how visitors understand the clinic, explore its treatments and find the next step towards contacting RIKOTA.
